Endpoints

GET/healthHealth check (no auth)
GET/api/appsList all installed apps
GET/api/apps/search?q=Search apps by name
POST/api/apps/launchLaunch app — body: {path} or {name}
POST/api/mediabody: {action: play|pause|stop|next|previous}
GET/api/brightnessGet current brightness level
POST/api/brightnessbody: {delta: ±1 step or 0.0–1.0 absolute}
POST/api/dndbody: {enabled: boolean}
GET/api/raycastCheck if Raycast is installed
POST/api/raycastLaunch Raycast — body: {query?: string}
